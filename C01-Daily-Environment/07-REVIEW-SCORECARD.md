- False pickup lights.
- Fake track geometry.
- Center too busy.
- HUD conflict.
- Red hazard-like forms.
- Gold coin-like forms.
- Cyan Shield-like forms.
- Wrong perspective.
- Major foreground occlusion.
- Overly generic AI look.
- Style incompatible with Rush Pi.

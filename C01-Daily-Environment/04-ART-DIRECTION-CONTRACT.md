# Art-direction contract

## Positive brief

Create an opaque vertical mobile environment, close to 9:16, for a premium arcade sci-fi runner. Use refined prismatic architecture: dark indigo/dark-violet ceramic and smoked-glass structural planes, subtle facets, satin gold details, violet emissive seams, and sparse cyan atmospheric depth. The visual world should frame a large readable central corridor from lateral side walls and distant depth structures.

The intended feeling is finished, architectural, spatial, and deliberate at 414px gameplay width. Materials should support the existing violet/gold Chain Block vocabulary and the Home/Daily environments without copying their exact pixels.

## Mandatory absence

The plate is **background/environment only**. It contains no UI, text, HUD, menus, logos, fake token marks, characters, player, Chain Blocks, hazards, pickups, gameplay objects, lane markings, or painted gameplay track.

It must also contain no bright collectible-looking dots in the lane area; red hazard-like forms; coin-like circular lights; Shield-like cyan pickups; gold collectible orbs; fake lane rails; fake road edges; fake portals; path-blocking spaceships; city traffic; signage; price symbols; crypto charts; currency imagery; Pi logos; Bitcoin logos; or generic finance imagery.

Avoid overly busy cyberpunk, fantasy temples, cartoon/anime treatment, photoreal city streets, random glowing circles, excessive lens flare, fog that destroys contrast, and huge foreground elements.

## Non-negotiable separation

Generated art supplies atmosphere only. It must sit behind the existing road (future target depth −20) and never establish a collision edge, lane boundary, path choice, token target, or gameplay timing cue. The road geometry, rails, chevrons, three lanes, player and all objects remain code-rendered and authoritative.
